About me

A student from Bishkek. I enjoy figuring out how things work—from a circuit board on a desk to a race car on the track. Here, I collect things that resonate with me and projects I've created myself.

And to get down to business:

I'm studying computer systems and complexes—covering microcontrollers, electronics, and programming. Alongside my studies, I create short videos and projects for my portfolio. For me, it's not just about achieving a result; it's about understanding why it works.
