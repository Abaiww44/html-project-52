Hobby

Motorsport

Formula 1, endurance racing and the engineering behind lap time.

What I follow

Formula 1 history and technical analysis: how regulations shape cars, and how cars shape careers.
Also endurance racing, especially Le Mans.

Debates I like

Driver or car?

A great car can hide an average driver, and a great driver can hide a weak car. I look for the cases where the gap is visible, for example in the career of Fernando Alonso.

How to measure greatness

Wins, poles and podiums each tell a different story, so one number never ranks drivers fairly. Nelson Piquet, a three-time world champion, is a driver I think is underrated.

New rules, new cars

The 2026 Formula 1 regulations bring active aerodynamics. I'm interested in how it changes racing, not only speed.

Cars I keep coming back to

Mercedes W11: the 2020 Formula 1 car with its dual-axis steering system.
Porsche 918 Spyder: a plug-in hybrid hypercar.
Nissan GT-R R35: all-wheel drive and a twin-turbo V6.
