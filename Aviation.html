Aviation

From airliners to fighters: every shape on an aircraft has a reason.

What interests me

Civil and military aviation, and military technology in general: aircraft, tanks, missiles.
Airliners show how engineers balance efficiency, safety and cost. Military aircraft show what happens when performance comes first.

Questions I like to break down

Why are wings swept back?

Close to the speed of sound, drag rises sharply. A swept wing delays that rise, so fast aircraft can fly efficiently.

How does a turbofan work?

A large fan pushes air around the engine core as well as through it. In modern airliner engines most of the thrust comes from this bypass air, which makes them quieter and more economical.

Why do stealth aircraft look that way?

Flat, angled surfaces and smooth edges reflect radar energy away from the source instead of straight back to it.
