Hobby

technique

Electronics, microcontrollers and building things that work.

What I do

I study microcontrollers, electronics and programming, and I solder and assemble circuits myself.
The goal is not only a working result, but understanding why it works.

Current build: sim racing PC

The platform

AM4 with a Ryzen 7 5700X3D. The platform is older but cheap, and the extra L3 cache of the 5700X3D helps in games.

The details

RAM, motherboards, power supply internals and silent cooling. Quiet matters when the PC stands next to you while you race.

Where I find parts

I buy some electronics secondhand on Chinese platforms such as Xianyu, through forwarding services.
It is cheaper, but you have to check the seller, the photos and the shipping carefully.
